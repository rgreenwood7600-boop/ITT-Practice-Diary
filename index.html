Register as a trainee

Registration is for trainees. It opens your own diary. Use a username, not an email address. Staff cannot register here.

The diary stores your name, rank, section, instrument and username, and the comments written each month. It does not ask for an email address or a service number. You can read your own diary. ITT staff can read it, write the staff comments, update your details, and set a new password for you. There are no analytics on this page.
